12Sharing a Project

12.1. The App allows a Project Owner to share a selected Project with one or more people through Apple's CloudKit service. A Project can only be shared as a whole — it is not possible to share selected stages or individual entries; the content and files excluded from a share are listed in clauses 12.6 and 12.7. Sharing requires an Account and an active iCloud account. The sample Project in Demo mode cannot be shared.

12.2. Sharing is voluntary and is initiated by the Project Owner in Apple's system share sheet, where the Project Owner chooses the people to invite and how the invitation is sent. The invitation contains the name of the Project. Only invited people have access, and they obtain it after accepting the invitation. Accepting an invitation requires an iCloud account on the device; ongoing synchronisation of changes in a Shared Project requires being signed in to the App.

12.3. The Project Owner decides with whom to share the Project and is responsible for that choice and for being entitled to disclose all content and personal data contained in the Project. Collaborators receive the Shared Project's data, including third-party data entered by the Project Owner — in particular contractor contacts with telephone numbers, email addresses, ratings and notes, the address and the plot or property details, expenses, the budget and expense attachments. The Project Owner should share the Project only with people to whom they are allowed to disclose this information.

12.4. Each Collaborator's permission level — view or edit — is set by the Project Owner in Apple's system share sheet and may be changed by the Project Owner at any time. The App applies these settings: a Collaborator with view permission may browse the Shared Project but cannot change it; a Collaborator with edit permission may add, change and delete its content. The permission applies to the whole Shared Project; the App does not support separate permissions for individual modules or individual entries. A change of permissions and a revocation of access take effect after the Collaborator's device synchronises.

12.5. As a rule, the App sends changes made by a participant with edit permission to the Shared Project and makes them available to the other participants on synchronisation. When the same entry is changed on more than one device, the App as a rule keeps one version of it — the one saved or sent later — as a whole, without merging the changes; the other version is replaced and cannot be restored. For some kinds of entries (for example albums and stage file descriptions) a later change may not be applied on devices that already have the entry. The App does not guarantee that every change or deletion will be sent and reflected on the devices of all participants.

12.6. The following are not shared as part of a Shared Project: finance form and financial profile data, notification settings, identifiers of events in the private iOS Calendar, files from the "Documents" tab, journal entry photos and the content of files added in a stage's files section (only their name and type are shared — clause 12.7). The App does not save in a Shared Project the name or email address provided by Apple at sign-in. The remaining content of the Shared Project, including the personal data it contains (clause 12.3), is shared with Collaborators, subject to clauses 12.5 and 12.7. The list of share participants is kept and displayed by Apple in the system share sheet.

12.7. As part of a Shared Project the App may transfer files: expense attachments, Project and stage covers, stage backgrounds and album photos — up to 50 MB each. Some photos are transferred in their original form, together with the metadata saved by the device, such as the date or location. For files added in a stage's files section, only their name and type are transferred. Photos added to an album after the share was created, and photos added by a Collaborator, may not be transferred to the other participants. The App does not guarantee that every file will be transferred, or that it will be available or displayed on every participant's device.

12.8. The Project Owner may at any time revoke a Collaborator's access or stop sharing in Apple's system share sheet. The Collaborator then loses access to the Project's current data. Deleting a Shared Project by the Project Owner does not by itself stop the share and does not delete all of its data from the Project Owner's iCloud — the remaining data and files may still be accessible to Collaborators; the Project Owner should therefore stop sharing before deleting a Shared Project. A copy of the data downloaded earlier may remain on the Collaborator's device until it is removed by the App or by the Collaborator; after access is revoked, the App blocks editing of that copy. The Provider cannot remove such a copy remotely. Stopping sharing does not by itself delete the Project's data from the Project Owner's iCloud.

12.9. A Collaborator may leave a Shared Project at any time by removing it from the Project list in the App (the "Remove local copy" command); the App then leaves the share and removes the saved data of that Project from the Collaborator's device. Some previously downloaded files (in particular stage covers and backgrounds) may remain in the App's storage until the App is removed from the device. Content and files added by a Collaborator become part of the Project Owner's Project and remain in the Project Owner's iCloud also after the Collaborator leaves the share; to delete them, the Collaborator should do so before leaving the share or ask the Project Owner. Deleting an entry in the App does not always delete all related data and files from the Project Owner's iCloud (for example the attachment file of a deleted expense). Leaving a Shared Project does not transfer ownership of the Project. The "Delete account and all data" feature does not stop or leave shares (clause 18.2).

12.10. The App does not provide: transfer of Project ownership, a change history, information about who authored an entry, notifications about changes made by other participants, real-time synchronisation, or recovery of data after a Shared Project has been deleted or the Project Owner has revoked access.

12.11. Users must not share content that is unlawful, infringes third-party rights or contains personal data that they are not authorised to disclose.

12.12. Project sharing relies on Apple services. Its availability may depend on the user's Apple account, iCloud configuration, internet connection, the availability of Apple services, and the versions of the system and the App installed on the device.
